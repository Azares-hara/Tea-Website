About Us

At the Tea House in Shillong, India, we pride ourselves at being the go-to destination for tea lovers and relief giver for outstanding look-outs of the wonderful city. We are a cozy resident aiming to provide the best relief medicine as nature's gift, tea! Brewed from various tea plants, concocted with excellent quality and taste-bud-enticing ingredients. Our customers can relax and unwind at this shelter in comfort.
